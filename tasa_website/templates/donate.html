TASA is always looking to bring the best experience to its members and to the local Berkeley community. As a student-run organization, we rely on the generosity of our members, alumni, and supporters to fund the events, families, and traditions that make TASA a home away from home. Every contribution — big or small — helps us keep doing what we love.
