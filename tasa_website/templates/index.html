Welcome to TASA @ Berkeley

TASA is more than a club — it’s a family.

We are the Taiwanese American Student Association, a social and cultural student organization at UC Berkeley celebrating everything Taiwanese American. Find us on Upper Sproul, Tuesday through Thursday, 11am–2pm.

Game nights, mahjong, picnics, water balloon fights, trips into San Francisco — we host events almost every week of the semester, big and small.

{{ heading('All are welcome!') }}

Our members come from vastly different backgrounds, and you don’t have to be Taiwanese to join — all you need is a heart for Taiwanese and Taiwanese American culture. We hope you’ll find your family here too.

{{ heading('Events and activities') }}

In line with our mission of fostering close friendships and greater appreciation for Taiwanese and Taiwanese-American culture, we host a wide range of events almost every week in the semester, from making traditional Taiwanese foods and playing mahjong to exploring the streets of San Francisco. TASA also holds events in collaboration with other groups on and off campus, including the Taiwanese Student Association, TAO at UC Davis, and the Global Leadership Organization. Be sure to join us at our next one!

{{ heading('Our history') }}

TASA was founded in 2003 to provide a way for Taiwanese-American students at Berkeley to connect with and understand their Taiwanese heritage and identity. Since then, TASA has grown to accommodate all students on campus interested in Taiwanese and Taiwanese-American culture. We’ve grown and changed much over the years, but our mission to promote community, awareness, and friendship among the student body has remained the same. We love hosting fun events for all of our members and look forward to making more TASA history this semester!
