{{ heading('Fun Events and Activities') }}

From Game nights and picnics to trips into San Francisco, we host a wide range of events almost every week of the semester! In line with our mission of fostering close friendships and greater appreciation for Taiwanese and Taiwanese-American culture, we also collaborate with other groups on and off campus, including the Taiwanese Student Association, TAO at UC Davis, and the Global Leadership Organization. {{ heading('Our history') }}

TASA was founded in 2003 to provide a way for Taiwanese-American students at Berkeley to connect with and understand their Taiwanese heritage and identity. Since then, TASA has grown to accommodate all students on campus interested in Taiwanese and Taiwanese-American culture. We’ve grown and changed much over the years, but our mission to promote community, awareness, and friendship among the student body has remained the same. We love hosting fun events for all of our members and look forward to making more TASA history this semester!
